sequenceDiagram
    actor User as ユーザー
    participant View as ConsoleView
    participant Controller as HistoryController
    participant UseCase as ShowAnswerHistoryUseCase
    participant Service as ShowAnswerHistoryService
    participant HistoryRepo as AnswerHistoryRepository
    participant DB as Database

    User->>Controller: 解答履歴確認を選択する
    Controller->>UseCase: execute(command)
    UseCase->>Service: execute(command)

    Service->>HistoryRepo: findByUserId(userId)
    HistoryRepo->>DB: SELECT * FROM answer_histories WHERE user_id = ?
    DB-->>HistoryRepo: 解答履歴一覧
    HistoryRepo-->>Service: List<AnswerHistory>

    alt 解答履歴が存在しない
        Service-->>Controller: ShowAnswerHistoryResult(false, "解答履歴はありません")
        Controller->>View: showMessage(message)
        View-->>User: 解答履歴なしメッセージを表示する
        Controller->>View: showUserMenu()
    else 解答履歴が存在する
        Service-->>Controller: ShowAnswerHistoryResult(true, histories)
        Controller->>View: showAnswerHistories(histories)
        View-->>User: 解答履歴一覧を表示する
        Controller->>View: showUserMenu()
    end
